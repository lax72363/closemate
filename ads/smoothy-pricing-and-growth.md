# Smoothy — Pricing & Growth Plan

## Market reference (2026)
- Cleo: $5.99–$14.99/mo
- Rocket Money: $7–$14/mo (pay-what-you-want)
- Monarch: $14.99/mo or $99.99/yr
- YNAB: ~$14.99/mo
- Pure auto-save (Qapital, old Digit): historically $3–$12/mo

Smoothy's edge: it ACTS (moves money automatically), not just tracks. That
justifies sitting mid-pack, not at the bottom.

## Recommended pricing

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | 1 auto-split rule, 1 linked account, basic dashboard |
| Pro  | $7.99/mo or $59/yr (~38% off) | Unlimited splits, multiple accounts, adaptive splits for irregular income, goals, alerts |
| Plus (later) | $14.99/mo | Investing routing, couples/shared accounts, priority support |

Notes:
- $7.99 Pro undercuts Monarch/YNAB ($14.99), matches Rocket Money's floor.
- Push annual ($59) to lock cash and reduce churn.
- Early-access "3 months Pro free" converts cleanly to $7.99/mo or $59/yr.
- Keep Free genuinely useful to get users past the "connect my bank" barrier.

## Growth levers
1. Referral loop: give a friend 1 month Pro, get 1 month free.
2. Savings milestones + shareable win cards ("Smoothy saved you $1,000").
3. Round-ups as a Pro feature (save spare change on top of splits).
4. Live "X of 1,000 spots left" waitlist counter for urgency.
5. 3–4 email onboarding drip to recover signups who didn't connect a bank.
6. Annual-first pricing UI (annual as default toggle).
7. "How Smoothy keeps your money safe" content page (trust + SEO).
