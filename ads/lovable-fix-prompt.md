# Lovable Prompt — Fix Smoothy Landing Page for Launch

Paste the block below into Lovable. It addresses the biggest pre-launch
conversion gaps: trust/security for a money-moving app, the "do you actually
move my money?" fear, pricing clarity, and objection handling.

---

You are improving the Smoothy landing page. Smoothy is an AI personal-finance
app: when a user's paycheck is deposited, Smoothy automatically moves a portion
into a separate account (savings, investing, goals). We are pre-launch and
running paid ads, so trust and clarity are critical.

Make these changes:

1. TRUST / SECURITY SECTION (highest priority)
   Add a dedicated "Is my money safe?" section near the top, right after the hero.
   Include these reassurances as clear, scannable cards or bullets:
   - Bank connections are read-and-route only — Smoothy can never spend your money
     or send it to anyone but YOUR own linked accounts.
   - We never store your bank login credentials. (If using Plaid/Teller/etc., name it.)
   - Bank-level 256-bit encryption.
   - You stay in control: pause, change splits, or withdraw anytime.
   - Link to a Privacy Policy and Terms (create placeholder pages if they don't exist).

2. CLARIFY "DO YOU MOVE MY MONEY?"
   Add one bold line in or just under the hero that answers the #1 visitor fear:
   "Smoothy moves money only between YOUR own accounts — never out to anyone else."

3. HOW IT WORKS (3 steps)
   Add a simple 3-step visual section:
   1) Connect your bank (read-only, secure)
   2) Set your splits once (e.g. 20% savings, 10% investing)
   3) Every paycheck auto-routes the moment it lands — hands-off.

4. PRICING TEASER
   Add a short, honest pricing line so "3 months Pro" makes sense:
   - Free plan: core auto-saving.
   - Pro plan: (list 2-3 perks). Early users get Pro free for 3 months.
   Even if final pricing isn't set, show the structure, not a blank.

5. FAQ SECTION (objection handling)
   Add an accordion FAQ with these questions:
   - Is my money safe?
   - Does Smoothy ever move money out to other people? (No.)
   - What banks do you support?
   - What does it cost after the free period?
   - Can I pause or cancel anytime?
   - How is my data protected?

6. CTA CONSISTENCY
   Use one primary CTA everywhere: "Start free". Repeat it in the hero, after
   How It Works, and after the FAQ. Keep the "First 1,000 users · 3 months Pro ·
   No credit card" microcopy under each CTA button.

7. POLISH
   - Keep the existing mascot and paycheck calculator — they're strong, leave them.
   - Mobile-first: ensure all new sections stack cleanly on phones.
   - Keep tone confident but honest. No fake reviews, no guaranteed-returns claims.

Do not change the brand colors or the core hero headline unless needed for the
clarity line above.
