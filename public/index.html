Questions agents ask us

Will it say something wrong or make things up?

CloseMate is instructed never to invent listings, prices, or legal/financial advice. When it doesn't know, it says you'll follow up — which is exactly the behavior that captures the lead.

Does it work on my website platform?

Yes. It's a single script tag, so it works on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy, kvCORE, Placester, and custom sites. We install it for you.

What happens when a lead comes in?

You get an instant email (or SMS via your CRM/Zapier) with everything the assistant learned: contact info, buy/sell intent, area, budget, timeline, and a hot/warm/cold rating.

Is it obviously a bot?

It's honest — if asked, it says it's your AI assistant and a human will follow up. Visitors don't mind; they mind waiting 6 hours for an answer.
