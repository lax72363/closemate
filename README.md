## The #1 business: Form 5500-EZ Deadline Rescue ("Solo401k Rescue")

A done-with-you filing concierge for solo 401(k) owners, sold against a hard IRS
deadline **21 days away**:

- Every solo 401(k) with **>$250k in assets** must file Form 5500-EZ by **July 31, 2026**.
- The late penalty is **$250/day, capped at $150,000** — and most owners have never
  heard of the form, because brokerages won't file it (it's legally the owner's duty)
  and most CPAs decline it (not an income-tax form, not in their software).
- Flagship offer: **$199 "File-With-Me" session** — 30 minutes on Zoom, the customer
  files on their own screen (we never touch accounts), filed-or-free guarantee.
  $99 self-serve kit downsell; $449/yr late-filer amnesty upsell.
- **$1,000 = 5–6 sessions.** Near-zero startup cost, ~100% margin, buyers concentrated
  in known communities (Bogleheads, r/financialindependence, BiggerPockets, FinTwit),
  and a genuine business afterward (filings recur annually by law; Oct 15 extension
  season; CPA white-label channel; $99/yr reminder subscription; path to a solo-401k
  compliance platform).

**Winner's score: 86.7/100 — a 14.5-point lead over #2**, the largest gap in the table.

## Why not closemate (the existing idea)?

Closemate (AI chatbot for real-estate lead gen) ranked **#23 of 42**: real pain, real
AI leverage, but entrenched competitors (Structurely, Ylopo, Roof.ai at $150–$1,000+/mo)
and a B2B trust-and-demo sales cycle longer than 72 hours. It's re-sequenced, not
killed: it returns as the backup plan (productized "speed-to-lead install", $299 +
$99/mo) and the post-challenge roadmap, funded by rescue-season cash.
