  search. If you have 10 seconds: what was missing?" (Always refund instantly;
  a $12 dispute costs more than $12.)
- **"File won't open":** "Save the file to your computer first, then
  double-click it — it opens in your normal browser. It works offline. If it
  still doesn't open, reply with your browser name and I'll help."
- **"Is this really private?":** "Yes — open your browser's devtools Network
  tab and run a scan: zero requests. The whole product is one HTML file."
