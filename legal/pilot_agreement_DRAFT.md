# Denial Recovery Pilot Agreement — DRAFT

> ⚠️ **DRAFT for review by an attorney or university small-business legal clinic before any use.**
> Signed by the LLC's authorized adult member. Not legal advice.

**Parties:** [LLC NAME], a [STATE] limited liability company ("Company") and
[PRACTICE LEGAL NAME] ("Practice").

**1. Services.** Company will analyze Practice's denied insurance claims, prepare appeal and
corrected-claim packages for claims listed on **Exhibit A (Approved Claim Inventory)**, and track
outcomes. Practice (or its designated biller) reviews and submits all appeals; where Practice
grants portal access, Company may submit on Practice's behalf as its billing agent. A human
reviews every document before submission.

**2. Payments to Practice.** All payer payments are made directly to Practice's accounts.
**Company never receives, holds, or directs Practice funds.**

**3. Fees.** For each Exhibit A claim verified as paid (per Practice's own remittance files):
- Commercial claims: [20]% of the amount actually paid.
- Claims where percentage fees are restricted by law or payer rules: flat $[25] per recovered claim.
- No recovery, no fee. Company invoices monthly with per-claim evidence; terms Net 15.
- **Excluded entirely:** Medicare/Medicaid claims, patient-responsibility balances, claims outside Exhibit A.

**4. Term & termination.** Month-to-month; either party may terminate with 15 days' written
notice. Fees remain due on Exhibit A claims paid within 90 days after termination for appeals
submitted before termination.

**5. Privacy.** The parties will execute a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (attached) before
any transfer of protected health information. Company uses PHI only to perform the Services,
under minimum-necessary principles.

**6. No guarantees; no legal/clinical advice.** Recovery estimates are estimates. Company does
not provide legal, clinical, or coding advice; Practice retains responsibility for the accuracy
of its clinical documentation.

**7. Liability cap.** Company's aggregate liability is capped at fees paid by Practice in the
prior 3 months. [Attorney: review carve-outs for data breach.]
