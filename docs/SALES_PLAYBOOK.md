# CloseMate Sales Playbook

Target: solo agents and small teams who have a website and no chat/lead capture
on it (check their site first — 30 seconds — and mention what you saw).

Where to find them (all free):
- Google Maps: "realtor [city]" → most profiles link a personal site with email.
- Zillow/Realtor.com agent directories → agent websites → contact email.
- Facebook groups: "[City] Realtors", "Real Estate Agent Mastermind" etc.
- Local Realtor association member directories (public).

Rule: **personalize the first line** with something true about their site or a
recent listing. Everything else can be template.

---

- `[FirstName] — quick question about [theirsite.com]`
- `the leads your site is dropping`

>

> timeline. You'd get an email the moment a lead comes in.
>
> Here's a 60-second live demo on a sample agent site — click the chat bubble
> and pretend you're a buyer: [DEMO_URL]
>
> Setup is $299 + $99/mo, installed for you within 48 hours, and if it doesn't
> capture a single lead in 30 days I refund everything.
>
> Worth a look?
> [YourName]

> Hi [FirstName] — quick nudge. The average commission in [City] is roughly

## Reply → close script (works over email or DM)

1. They reply with interest → answer any question in ≤3 sentences, then:
   > "Easiest next step: I set it up on your site this week — $299 setup,
   > $99/mo, cancel anytime, and the 30-day first-lead guarantee means the
   > risk's on me. Here's the payment link: [STRIPE_LINK]. Once that's in I
   > just need your website login or your web person's email, and you're live
   > within 48h."
2. If hesitant → offer the demo *on their own site*: "Want me to show you what
   it looks like on [theirsite.com]? I can send a screenshot mockup today."
   (Screenshot their homepage, ask Claude to composite the widget, send it.)

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "AI will say something wrong" | It never invents listings or prices — when it doesn't know, it says *you'll* follow up, which is exactly what captures the lead. Try to trip it up in the demo. |
| "I already have a form" | Forms convert ~2% because nobody wants to fill a form at 11pm. A conversation converts several times better and tells you their budget and timeline, not just an email. |
| "Too expensive" | One closing pays for ~10 years of CloseMate. And there's the 30-day refund if it captures nothing. (Fallback: $149 setup this week.) |
| "I use Zillow leads" | Those cost $300-1500/mo for *shared* leads. This captures people already on *your* site who found *you* — exclusive by definition. |
| "Need to think about it" | Totally fine — the demo link stays live. Can I check back Friday? (Then actually check back Friday.) |
| "Is this a bot talking to me right now?" | Ha — no, I'm real, the bot works for *your* visitors. |

## Daily cadence (during the 72h)
- Hour 0–2: send 50 emails (batch 1) + join 3 FB groups.
- Hour 2–24: send 50 more, 20 DMs. Reply to everything within 15 min when awake.
- Day 2: follow-up email 2 to all of batch 1; 50 new emails.
- Day 3: breakup email to batch 1, follow-up 2 to batch 2, close open threads.

## Pricing card (canonical)
- $299 one-time setup + $99/month. Cancel anytime.
- 30-day guarantee: no lead captured → full refund.
- This-week fallback (use only to close a wavering buyer): $149 setup.
- Never discount the monthly. The monthly is the business.
